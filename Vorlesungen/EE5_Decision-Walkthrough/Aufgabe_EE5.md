## Aufgaben (Folientext)

1. Go through every second of the gameplay in your mind in real-time without skipping anything!
   - [ ] Imagine every (single, boring) step (5 Minuten Spielzeit = 5 Minuten Durchdenken)
   - [ ] Discuss decision scope and pacing
   - [ ] Do you know which actions are possible?
   - [ ] Are they part of a consistent and comprehensible system?
   - [ ] Do you know the possible future outcomes and consequences of your actions?
   - [ ] Where do you achieve full/partial predictability?
2. Consider information starvation vs. balance vs. glut
   - [ ] Do you have the right amount of knowledge to take a decision?
   - [ ] Where can you stage consequences of decisions? Where can you add or hide information?
3. - [ ] Where can you improve your game? Adjust your prototype accordingly
