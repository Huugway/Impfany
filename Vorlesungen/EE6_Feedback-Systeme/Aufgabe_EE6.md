## Aufgaben (Folientext)

1. After/besides concluding evaluation gulf audit (EE4) and information balancing (EE5), optimize your reward system:
   - [ ] Classify each planned feedback element with the four lenses (**timescale, function, modality, diegesis**)
   - [ ] Discuss where your feedback loop is positive/negative × rewarding/punishing and how this affects flow
   - [ ] Discuss where your feedback is controlling/informative and how this affects intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation
   - [ ] Match each feedback element with the three SDT needs (**competence, autonomy, relatedness**). Add feedback if a need is not addressed.
   - [ ] Discuss motivational effects (suitability, expected effectiveness, **no dark patterns**) from at least two of the groups:
     Chance & timing · Progress & goal · Pull & habit · Comparison & ending
2. - [ ] Add at least two feedback effects to your game accordingly
